Okay, this one's fun. When I first ran this game, I had it close to a coin flip. Tampa Bay is 3–1, Baker is playing well, and the Cowboys lost to the Texans last week. I get why the fans like the Bucs.

But every time I dig into the matchups, I end up in the same place: Dallas's pass rush against Baker Mayfield. It's the biggest mismatch on the field, and it's big enough to swing the whole game. So I'm on Dallas, 64%.

The matchup that decides it

Here's the number that jumped out at me: when Baker has a clean pocket, he completes 71% of his passes, which is top ten. When he's pressured, that drops to 48%, bottom five in the league. That's one of the biggest drop-offs of any starting quarterback.

And Dallas pressures quarterbacks on 41% of dropbacks, more than any team in football. Micah Parsons alone has 22 pressures in four games. Tampa Bay's line has given up the 6th-most pressure in the league, and their right tackle (Luke Goedeke) is questionable with a knee injury.

Quick explainerA "pressure" is any time the quarterback gets sacked, hit, or rushed into throwing early. It matters more than sacks alone, because hurried throws are where interceptions come from.

How Tampa Bay wins this

I'm at 64%, not 85%, and here's why. Tampa has a real path:

  • 1Get the ball out fast. If Baker throws quick (2.5 seconds or less), the pass rush never gets there. Tampa did this against Detroit in Week 2 and won by 10.
  • 2Run Bucky Irving early. Dallas is 20th against the run. A few early carries slow the rush down.
  • 3Win the turnover battle. Dallas has 7 giveaways in 4 games. Two short fields for Tampa and my whole case falls apart.
